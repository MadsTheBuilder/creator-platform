# Product

Ported from Creator_OS `PRODUCT.md` (2026-10-05) and trimmed to this repo.

## Register

product

## Users

Creators across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. They open the app to understand how their own channels are performing, then plan, storyboard, previs, edit and repurpose content. Not limited to one niche.

## Product Purpose

One web app that consolidates seven creator modules: Analytics, Trends & News, Planner, Script → Storyboard, 3D Previs, Video Edit, Repurpose. The opening screen is analytics: understanding channel performance comes first. Success is a creator trusting the numbers enough to decide what to make next.

## Brand Personality

Name: Content Engine. Voice: clear, useful, creator-controlled. Three words: calm, precise, warm.

## Anti-references

- Busy SaaS dashboards with a dozen colored widgets and decorative charts.
- Fabricated "growth" numbers, virality promises, hype copy.
- Blue as the main color (founder rejected it).

## Design Principles

- The creator owns the idea; the tool supports, never volunteers.
- Evidence, interpretation and prediction are visibly different. Missing metrics and disconnected services get explicit states, never invented values.
- Clean over clever: one quiet surface, motion only to confirm interaction.
- One module working end to end beats seven half-wired. Unbuilt modules are shown as "coming soon", not faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible keyboard focus everywhere, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks, icon-only controls carry accessible names.
