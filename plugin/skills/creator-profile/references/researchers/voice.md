# Brief: voice

**Goal:** how the creator talks, so a new script can follow their method without reusing their lines.

Read `_contract.md` first.

**Inputs:** every `captions/<id>.hook.<lang>.txt` (native language; trust it for phrasing), `captions/<id>.txt` (full transcript or translation; use it for structure and length), and `manifest.json` for durations.

**Produce:**
1. **Persona:** who speaks, to whom, on or off camera, register, tone, person and tense. Note any change by era.
2. **Language mix:** which language carries the grammar, which terms stay in which language, and code-switching patterns, with examples cited from the native samples.
3. **Signature connectors:** 8–15 recurring transition phrases or patterns. Quote at most 3–6 words each; these are patterns to imitate, not lines to reuse.
4. **Rhythm:** words per minute (words in the full transcript ÷ minutes; say it is approximate if translated), average words per sentence, questions per minute.
5. **Hook formulas:** one formula per analysed video, abstracted (e.g. "real case → 'if you think it is rare' → stat → 'what if it is you?'"), each tagged with its video id.
6. **Calls to action and endings:** type, frequency, and change by era.
7. **Script template:** a timed skeleton for the creator's most successful video type.
8. **Not assessed:** delivery, pitch, emotion and pauses cannot be judged from text. Also note the transcript accuracy issues you spotted.
