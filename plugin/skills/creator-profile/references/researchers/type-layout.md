# Brief: type-layout

**Goal:** typography, caption style, layout grammar, and an inventory of recurring graphic cards with their text slots.

Read `_contract.md` first.

**Inputs:** `frames/hook-*`, `frames/contact-*` and `frames/dense-*`. Extract full-size frames of signature graphics with ffmpeg when you need to read a typeface.

**Produce:**
1. **Typefaces:** display, body, serif and mono as observed (condensed grotesk, slab, typewriter…). Give the **closest Google Font** for each, and note weight, case and tracking.
2. **Captions:** position, size relative to the frame, case, emphasis method (colour word, highlight, box), words per caption, and whether they are always on.
3. **Layout grammar:** talking-head framing, picture-in-picture, split screens, full-frame takeovers, safe margins, and where text never goes (e.g. over the face). For vertical 9:16 sources (TikTok, Reels), note where on-screen text sits relative to the platform UI zones (the caption/username band at the bottom, the action buttons on the right) and whether the creator keeps text clear of them.
4. **Card inventory:** each recurring graphic (stat card, quote, newspaper, timeline, definition, lower third, chapter card…). For each give the frame citation, how often it appears, its HyperFrames tier (tier1 = opaque full frame: thesis, stat, section, quote, overview; tier2 = overlay: label, list, equation, definition, lower-third), and its **text slots** with rough max characters.
5. **Don'ts:** rounded corners, glass, emoji or whatever this creator never uses.
