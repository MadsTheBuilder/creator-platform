# Trying Content Engine as a guest

Open **https://worker-production-b2a3.up.railway.app/#guest**. You're signed in straight away, with no Google account needed, and the Playground opens.

## What you can do

The guest account starts as a copy of Madhu's workspace, and everything in it is yours to use:

- Open the projects and create new ones on either track (Production or Studio).
- Write and edit scripts and shot breakdowns.
- Use the Storyboard and the video editor.
- Plan videos in the Planner.
- Run Trends & News.
- Edit the styles.

## Good to know

- **The account is shared.** Everyone with the link works in the same account, so you'll see each other's projects and changes. Don't put anything private in it.
- **It can be reset at any time.** The reset puts it back to a fresh copy of Madhu's workspace, and that removes your changes.
- **Channels can't be connected here.** YouTube, Instagram and TikTok are off in the guest account, so the analytics Overview stays empty.
- **AI video uses your own Higgsfield account.**

## Optional: your own Claude and your PC

- **Your Claude or Codex.** The site writes scripts, breakdowns and Studio builds through your own assistant. Open the guest link first in the same browser, then follow the steps on the Connections page (see also `docs/mcp-setup.md`). The approval page uses the session you already have, so you won't need Google.
- **Your PC.** Blender blockouts and speech-to-text run on your own computer. In a project's 3D visual step, download and pair the PC helper. Every paired guest PC pulls from the same queue, so a job may run on someone else's PC.
